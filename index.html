How it works

  1. Add images by dragging them in, browsing, pasting (Ctrl+V) or entering a URL.
  2. Pick the language of the text in the image.
  3. Click Convert. Text is extracted using OCR right in your browser.
  4. Copy or download the result as a .txt file.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. There's no sign-up and no limit on how many images you convert.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

In Fast mode, no. Text recognition runs entirely in your browser. In AI mode, the image is sent securely to our server and an AI model to read it, and it isn't stored.

Why is the first conversion slower?

The OCR engine and language data load on first use, then get cached. Later conversions are much faster.

How can I get better accuracy?

Use sharp, well-lit images with straight text, choose the right language, and keep "Enhance image" on. Handwriting and stylised fonts are harder to read.
